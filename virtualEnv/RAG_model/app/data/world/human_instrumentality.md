The Human Instrumentality Project is NERV’s ultimate goal: to unify all human souls into one collective consciousness,
erasing loneliness, pain, and individuality.

While presented as “human evolution,” the project’s ethical implications are heavily debated.
Commander Ikari’s personal motivations may differ from SEELE’s original intentions.

The Evangelion Units and the Angels are both critical components in triggering this process,
each representing fragments of a divine system that humanity only partially understands.
