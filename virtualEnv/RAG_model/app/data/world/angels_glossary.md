# Glossary of Angels

**Angel** — A mysterious life form attempting to reach the entity known as Lilith, sealed beneath NERV HQ.
Each Angel has a unique structure, intelligence, and AT Field (Absolute Terror Field), a defensive barrier born from the soul.

**Sachiel (Third Angel)**  
First to attack Tokyo-3. Its AT Field repelled conventional weapons until Shinji Ikari successfully engaged using EVA Unit-01.

**Shamshel (Fourth Angel)**  
A serpentine creature emitting high-energy whips. Its defeat marked Shinji’s growing combat experience.

**Ramiel (Fifth Angel)**  
A geometric crystal-shaped Angel firing particle beams. Required a long-range sniper plan (Operation Yashima) to destroy.

# AT Field
The AT Field is a manifestation of the soul that separates individuals.  
Evangelions and Angels both generate AT Fields, symbolizing their emotional isolation and individuality.
